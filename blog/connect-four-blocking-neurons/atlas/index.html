Every neuron, by layer

The model reads a game as a list of column numbers and has 6 layers of 1,024 neurons. Each neuron has two sides, a positive and a negative pole, which usually mean different things. Each square is one neuron, coloured by the theme of the side that matches a game concept best. Click a square to open it, or set up a board and watch every neuron respond.

Brightness: how strongly each neuron fires on this board, against its own firing threshold. Stones alternate; the player to move, the other.

A map of all 12,288 poles

Every pole is placed by what the board looks like when it fires (which cells hold the mover's stones, the opponent's, or nothing, compared with usual), so poles that read similar board patterns sit together. Colour is the theme of the concept it matches best; grey dots match none well. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move, click a dot to open it.
